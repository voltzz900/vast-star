## Description

A content mod: data only, nothing to build. The goal at the end of a long game.

- **Core of the Deep** (Vast Depths' Stellarite, Infernium and Voidium blocks), **Core of Alloys** (a block of each of Vast Ores' nine alloys), **Core of Pressure** (Vast Compressed's VI and V tiers), **Core of Valor** (Vast Adventures' gems, seals and a Titan Pearl) and **Core of the Wild** (a totem, a dragon head, a sniffer egg, a heart of the sea, a trident, an enchanted golden apple and more).
- **Star Seed:** four nether stars, four echo shards and a heavy core.
- **Vast Star:** the five cores, the Star Seed and four end crystals. Nine make a **Block of Vast Star**, which shines and serves a beacon.
- English and Brazilian Portuguese; every sprite drawn for this mod, the star shimmering.

**Install Vast Depths, Vast Ores, Vast Compressed and Vast Adventures too.** Inspired by the All The Mods packs' ATM Star; nothing of it is used, not affiliated with or endorsed by All The Mods.
